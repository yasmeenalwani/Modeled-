# PROFESSIONAL PROFILE PAGE - REDESIGN
## Professional Card & Journey Visualization

This document outlines the complete redesign of the Professional Profile page to function as a comprehensive professional card showcasing their Modeled Management journey from emerging to experienced.

---

## DESIGN CONCEPT

### Core Philosophy
Transform the profile page from a simple form into a **premium professional card** that:
- Tells the professional's story and journey
- Showcases their growth on Modeled Management
- Highlights certifications, achievements, and expertise
- Acts as a portfolio showcase and business card
- Connects to all their work and services

---

### Experience Level System

#### Emerging (0-25 sessions)
- **Description:** Just starting on Modeled Management
- **Badge Color:** Soft Blue (#667eea)
- **Requirements:** Completed onboarding, active profile
- **Access:** Basic matching, all training materials

#### Developing (26-75 sessions)
- **Description:** Building experience and skills
- **Badge Color:** Warm Purple (#8B1E3F)
- **Requirements:** 
  - 25+ completed sessions
  - 4.5+ average rating
  - 2+ certifications
- **Access:** Priority matching, featured opportunities

#### Experienced (76-150 sessions)
- **Description:** Established professional with proven track record
- **Badge Color:** Deep Cherry (#A85A5A)
- **Requirements:**
  - 75+ completed sessions
  - 4.7+ average rating
  - 4+ certifications
  - 90%+ reliability score
- **Access:** Premium matching, mentorship opportunities

#### Expert (151+ sessions)
- **Description:** Top-tier professional and community leader
- **Badge Color:** Gold (#D4AF37)
- **Requirements:**
  - 150+ completed sessions
  - 4.9+ average rating
  - 5+ certifications
  - 95%+ reliability score
  - Featured portfolio
- **Access:** VIP matching, mentorship roles, brand partnerships

## VISUAL DESIGN

### Color Palette
- **Emerging:** Soft Blue (#667eea)
- **Developing:** Warm Purple (#8B1E3F)
- **Experienced:** Deep Cherry (#A85A5A)
- **Expert:** Gold (#D4AF37)
- **Background:** Cream (#FFFEF9)
- **Text:** Dark Brown (#4A2A1A)

### Typography
- **Headers:** "Alike" serif (elegant, professional)
- **Body:** System sans-serif (readable, modern)
- **Accents:** Uppercase, letter-spaced

### Layout Style
- **Card-based design** with soft shadows
- **Magazine-style** layout with clean spacing
- **Visual hierarchy** with clear sections
- **Responsive grid** for stats and certifications
