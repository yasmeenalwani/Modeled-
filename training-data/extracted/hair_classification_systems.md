# Hair Classification Systems Research

## 1. Andre Walker Hair Typing System (1A-4C)

The most widely adopted hair classification system, created in the 1990s by Oprah Winfrey's stylist Andre Walker.

### Type 1: Straight Hair
| Subtype | Texture | Description |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| 1A | Fine | Hard to hold a curl, tends to be oily, hard to damage |
| 1B | Medium | Has much body (more volume, more full) |
| 1C | Coarse | Hard to curl (bone straight) |

### Type 2: Wavy Hair
| Subtype | Texture | Description |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| 2A | Loose waves | Can accomplish various styles. Loose "S" pattern. Hair sticks close to the head |
| 2B | Defined waves | A bit resistant to styling. More defined "S" pattern. Tends to be frizzy |
| 2C | Wide waves | Hair has wider waves. Resistant to styling. Tends to be frizzy |

### Type 3: Curly Hair
| Subtype | Texture | Description |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| 3A | Loose curls | Thick and full with much body. Definite curl pattern. Can have combination texture |
| 3B | Tight curls | Medium amount of space of the curls. Can have combined texture |
| 3C | Corkscrews | Tight curls in corkscrews. Very tightly curled |

### Type 4: Kinky-Coily Hair
| Subtype | Texture | Description |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| 4A | Defined coil | Tightly coiled. Very defined O-shaped pattern |
| 4B | Z coil | Tightly coiled. Less defined kink pattern. Z-shaped pattern |
| 4C | Tight coil | Tightly coiled. Almost no visible defined kink pattern unless seen up close. Very tight O-shaped pattern |

## 2. LOIS Hair Typing System

An alternative system that incorporates strand pattern, strand thickness, and hair texture.

### Strand Pattern (L.O.I.S.)
- **L**: Sharp angles and bends, hardly any curvature
- **O**: Curls or coils creating O-shaped pattern
- **I**: Straight strands, no significant bends/curls/coils
- **S**: Curves creating variations of the letter S

### Strand Size (Diameter)
- **Fine**: Thinner than sewing thread
- **Medium**: Same diameter as sewing thread
- **Coarse**: Thicker than sewing thread

### Hair Texture (Surface)
| Texture | Sheen | Shine | Water Absorption | Frizz | Chemical Processing |
|---------|-------|-------|------------------|-------|---------------------|
| Thready | Low-moderate | High when stretched | Quick absorb, quick loss | Low | Quick processing |
| Wiry | Sparkly | Low | Water beads up, resistant | Low | Resistant |
| Cottony | Low | Low (high when stretched) | Accepts readily, slow absorb | High | Normal |
| Spongy | High | Low (even stretched) | Absorbs slowly | Compact high | Normal |
| Silky | Low | High | Quick absorb, easily saturated | Variable | Fine strands |

## 3. FIA Hair Typing System

Combines elements of Andre Walker system with additional testing for enhanced hair profile.

### Components:
1. **Curl Pattern** (1-4 with A-C subtypes)
2. **Strand Thickness** (Fine/Medium/Coarse)
3. **Overall Volume** (Thin/Normal/Thick)

## 4. Scientific Hair Measurements

### Porosity
- **Low**: Tight cuticle, water beads up, slow absorption
- **Medium**: Balanced cuticle, normal absorption
- **High**: Raised/damaged cuticle, quick absorption and loss

### Porosity Testing Methods:
1. **Float Test**: Clean hair in water - floats (low), sinks slowly (medium), sinks fast (high)
2. **Slide Test**: Run fingers up strand - smooth (low), slightly rough (medium), rough (high)
3. **Gas Sorption**: Lab method measuring total pore volume

### Elasticity
- **Low**: Snaps easily when stretched
- **Medium**: Stretches and returns with some effort
- **High**: Stretches significantly and returns to original length

### Density
- **Low**: Scalp easily visible through hair
- **Medium**: Some scalp visible
- **High**: Scalp not visible through hair

## 5. Hair Morphology Metrics (Scientific)

### Cross-Sectional Shape
- Round → Straight hair
- Oval → Wavy hair
- Elliptical → Curly hair
- Flat → Coily/kinky hair

### Diameter Measurements
- Fine: < 60 micrometers
- Medium: 60-80 micrometers
- Coarse: > 80 micrometers

### Curl Geometry Parameters
- Curl radius
- Wave amplitude
- Wavelength
- Helix pitch
