## Side-by-Side Quick Comparison

| Feature | Mockup 1: Magazine Cover | Mockup 2: Tiles Hub | Mockup 3: Storyline |
|---------|-------------------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| **Hero Style** | Bold headline + tagline | Stats bar only | Story headline + cover |
| **Stats Display** | 3 vertical chips (right) | 4 equal cards (top) | Embedded in hero/story |
| **Content Layout** | 2-column grid | 3×2 tile grid | Timeline + suggestions |
| **Visual Style** | Editorial, magazine-like | Organized, modular | Narrative, story-driven |
| **Primary Focus** | Model card + sessions | Information density | Timeline + next steps |
| **Interaction Style** | Magazine browsing | Tile exploration | Story progression |
| **Best For** | Impact & inspiration | Quick info access | Engagement & journey |
