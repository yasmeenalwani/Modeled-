## ⚖️ Legal Considerations

### Patent vs. Trade Secret Decision Matrix

| Factor | Patent | Trade Secret |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **Disclosure Required** | Yes (public after 18 months) | No |
| **Protection Duration** | 20 years | Indefinite (if kept secret) |
| **Enforcement** | Public record, easier to enforce | Private, harder to prove |
| **Cost** | $10K - $20K per patent | Minimal (documentation only) |
| **Best For** | Novel, non-obvious inventions | Proprietary formulas, algorithms |

### Recommendations
- **Patent:** Matching engine, workflow automation (if novel)
- **Trade Secret:** Exact scoring formulas, pricing algorithms, proprietary calculations
