### 7. Your Privacy Rights

#### 7.1 Access and Portability
You have the right to:
- Access your personal information
- Receive a copy of your data in a portable format
- Request correction of inaccurate data

#### 7.2 Deletion
You have the right to request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements.

#### 7.3 Opt-Out Rights
You have the right to:
- Opt out of marketing communications
- Opt out of certain data processing (where applicable)
- Manage cookie preferences

#### 7.4 California Privacy Rights (CCPA)
California residents have additional rights:
- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to delete personal information
- Right to opt-out of sale of personal information
- Right to non-discrimination

#### 7.5 European Privacy Rights (GDPR)
European residents have additional rights:
- Right to access
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object
- Rights related to automated decision-making
