## 📝 HOW TO SAVE FILES

### **Before Adding to Project:**
1. **Name files clearly:**
   - ✅ `modeled-management-logo.png`
   - ✅ `pearl-background.jpg`
   - ❌ `IMG_1234.jpg` (not descriptive)

2. **Use lowercase with hyphens:**
   - ✅ `hero-image.jpg`
   - ✅ `card-icon.png`
   - ❌ `Hero Image.jpg` (spaces cause issues)

3. **Organize by type:**
   - Logos → `assets/logos/`
   - Backgrounds → `assets/backgrounds/`
   - Photos/Icons → `assets/images/`
