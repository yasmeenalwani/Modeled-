## RECOMMENDATIONS

1. **Start with MVP Launch priorities** - Get essential copy polished for launch
2. **Create a brand voice guide first** - Ensure consistency
3. **Batch similar content** - Write all emails together, all forms together
4. **Use templates** - Create reusable templates for similar content types
5. **Get legal review early** - Terms/Privacy need legal review
6. **A/B test critical copy** - CTAs, headlines, conversion points
7. **Prioritize user-facing copy** - Portal copy before admin copy
8. **Consider hiring a copywriter** - 1,200+ pieces is a lot for one person
