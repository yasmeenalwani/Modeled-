## Service-Type Color Differentiation with Muted Complementary Palette

**Generated:** January 6, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Focus:** Pro Portal Booked/Sessions Page Redesign

---

## DESIGN BRIEF

**Current Issues:**
- Hard to understand what services are being booked
- No visual differentiation between service types
- Cards look similar, making it difficult to scan quickly

**Requirements:**
- Visual differentiation by service type using card colors
- Light muted complementary palette to cherry/pink/cream warm nexus vibes
- Clear service identification at a glance
- Maintain luxury but accessible aesthetic
- Multiple style options for review
